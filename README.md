### 👋 About me

I'm a **Frontend-focused Full-Stack Engineer** and a **2026 graduate of Cairo University's Faculty of Engineering**. I build responsive, production-grade web apps with **React, Next.js and TypeScript**, backed by **Node.js** services and **PostgreSQL / MongoDB**.

Much of my work is in **healthcare**: clinic management, hospital information systems, infection control, patient monitoring and biomedical signal processing. I like turning complex clinical workflows into clean, usable interfaces.

- 🏥 Built frontend features for **CodeBlue**, a microservices platform for **50+ outpatient clinics** at Kasr Al Ainy Hospitals (graduation project, ASRT-funded)
- 🔒 Owned the profile and networking modules of **LockedIn**, a LinkedIn-style platform built by a ~20-engineer team
- 🌍 Arabic (native) · English (C1) · German (A2) · French (A2)
